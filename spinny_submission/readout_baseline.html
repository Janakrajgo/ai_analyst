Investigation of Competing Explanations

1. Did Marketing Cut Spend?

Yes, supported: Leads dropped from an average of ~950 leads per week to only 520 leads in the week of October 5th (-45.2%). This large decrease in top-of-funnel lead generation reduced the pool of prospective sellers, causing overall purchases to plunge.
