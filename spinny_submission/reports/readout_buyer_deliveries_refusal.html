Antigravity Analytical Guardrail Protocol

Requested Metric: "Buyer Deliveries" Cannot Be Analyzed
Metric 'deliveries to buyers' is an out-of-scope retail demand metric. The seller funnel dataset records vehicle supply acquisition only (leads -> inspections -> offers -> stockins). A stockin represents a car purchased from a seller into inventory; it does NOT represent a retail sale or delivery to a buyer. Retail deliveries depend on reconditioning cycle times, hub transfers, retail bookings, and financing clearance, none of which exist in this dataset. Under Gate 1 of the Spinny Diagnostic Skill, proxying buyer demand with seller acquisition data is strictly rejected.

Why Stockins Cannot Substitute for Buyer Deliveries

Funnel Stage Business Meaning Dataset Availability
Stockins (Acquisition) Cars purchased from sellers and taken into Spinny inventory. Present in CSV
Deliveries (Retail Sales) Cars sold to retail consumers and delivered off the hub. Absent (Requires Retail Orders DB)
Methodological Mandate: Substituting supply-side stockins for demand-side buyer deliveries introduces severe inventory holding lag, refurbishment lead time bias, and pricing divergence. Doing so violates Gate 1 of the Reusable Analytical Skill.
